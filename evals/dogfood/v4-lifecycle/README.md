# A complete installed experiment workflow, with its failed attempts preserved

The final fresh-client exercise passed all eight checks in the prospectively
frozen [protocol](protocol.md). A preparer saved a real build decision's finite
model and experiment; a separate fresh resumer recovered it using only the
notebook and decision ID, executed the actual bounded check, previewed its sourced
observation, applied it once, and produced a conditional revised brief.

The path took eight product operations in [run 3](run-3/README.md). Earlier
failures were retained and corrected at their causal layer:

| Attempt | Changed treatment | Product attempts | Result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| [Run 1](run-1/README.md) | Original lifecycle guidance and capture helper | 11 | Failed effort bound: redundant full and pre/post-preview reads. |
| [Run 2](run-2/README.md) | Clarified full write receipts and revision/byte verification | 9, including one failed launch | Failed effort bound: eight successful operations plus a mistyped executable path. |
| [Run 3](run-3/README.md) | Same product and guidance; exact capture aliases and short driver path | 8 | All eight fixed checks passed; no failures or retries. |

All runs used the same 0.6.0 wheel, with SHA-256
`ed31585b6f3326b018874d8dcb47a5dfb6e8186cf0158141a4dd84657d9814ff`.
Each used a new preparer/resumer pair and notebook. The semantic task, input
model, actual check, and acceptance thresholds stayed fixed. The second and
third interventions were registered before their participants ran; no participant
received earlier results, hidden grading criteria, or coaching toward a pass.

The guidance correction belonged in the existing notebook and experiment
references. The repeated path-construction failure belonged in the capture
helper, repaired through BDD and failing/passing regression tests. Its aliases
resolve only explicitly named executables from the frozen manifest and retain
both requested and actual argv. Unknown paths still fail; no fuzzy repair or
automatic retry hides a failed attempt.

## What this proves

The installed tool can retain a plan, recover it after a cold handoff, incorporate
an actual sourced observation through a read-only preview and atomic apply, and
preserve model lineage and decision-changing arithmetic. The final run retained
the prior, one observed outcome, current posterior, revised preference, and
advisory/source-verification boundaries. A separate root readback check confirms
the underlying state alongside the ordered criterion assessment.

The final [participant brief](run-3/resume/final-brief.md) connects that evidence
to a conditional review recommendation, a material alternative, uncertainty, and
reversal conditions. The generic notebook brief still reports `needs_work` when
narrative categories have not been authored. Full audit output remains lengthy,
and historical receipt flags and preparation constraints require context. Those
remaining usability limits are recorded rather than hidden with extra notes.

## Limits and complete costs

These are author-visible local first-consumer observations. They are not an
untouched holdout, a native host-discovery test, or a controlled demonstration of
general behavioral superiority. The 91-command earlier exercise performed a
different task; no percentage improvement is claimed against it.

Each run executed the same three-case finite arithmetic check once. Repeated
checks on the same artifact are not independent reliability evidence, and their
illustrative posteriors must not be combined. The models' priors, payoffs, and
likelihoods are caller judgments, not calibrated measurements.

Every run retains target attempts, help/setup/verification work, captured reads,
capture-helper launches, and disclosed wrapper failures. In total, the three
exercises retained 63 target attempts: 62 launched processes and one failed
launch. Two additional wrapper failures occurred before capture. This complete
attempt history is not erased by the final pass. Unknown outer host details and
agent work are outside target-process timing and are not reconstructed.

See each manifest for exact wheel, source, guidance, task, and check fingerprints;
each assessment for the fixed ordered checks; and each trace for actual execution
evidence. No registry release, vendor account installation, external action
permission, or universal decision-quality claim follows from these observations.
