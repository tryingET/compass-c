## Why the effort gate failed

The preparer saved a compact plan then fetched a separate full audit view. The
resumer fetched current state before and after preview in addition to retaining
identical database hashes. Normal notebook guidance requested a `get` after
mutation while the experiment guide encouraged carrying returned revisions.
The participants disclosed this conflict. A full initial write response and
explicit guidance about preview revision/byte evidence can avoid these redundant
reads without reducing verification.

Other friction remains visible: the general notebook brief reports `needs_work`
because narrative note categories are empty; the final human brief supplies the
conditional interpretation without claiming structural completeness. The stored
observation readback has `replayed: true` and `applied: false`, requiring readers
to distinguish it from the original successful apply receipt. A pending-plan
summary retains historical no-test values after observation while clearly
marking its original proposal consumed.
