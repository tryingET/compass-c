## 1. Package scope and evidence

This is an operational reference for the COMPASS advisory skill, not a report of
an experiment. The package provides Markdown guidance, a local decision notebook,
and seven conditional arithmetic helpers. It does not contain an action gate,
protected audit service, resource reservation system, online shift detector, or
independent specialist agents. Earlier COMPASS gate experiments are separate
artifacts and are not evidence that those capabilities exist here.

The CLI is the source of truth for supported operations. Its local regression
suite checks implementation behavior, not the skill's effectiveness on a model.
The methodology used to engineer this version is pinned in the full toolkit's
engineering/source_register.json. Core decision principles are recommendations;
parameter values are supplied assumptions unless grounded in actual evidence.

## 2. Choosing a decision criterion

Separate beliefs, endorsed values, and authority. Evidence can change a belief;
it cannot silently redefine a protected constraint or authorize an action.
Apply the actual constraints before comparing feasible options. Include delay or
inaction when relevant, but do not presume either is safe.

Use expected value only with defensible probabilities and comparable payoffs.
Use worst-case or regret comparisons only over a stated scenario set. Show the
price paid for that protection and a plausible failure outside the set. Where
values conflict without an endorsed common scale, expose the tradeoff rather than
inventing weights. The compare helper supplies arithmetic, not admissibility,
legitimacy, or a universal decision criterion.

## 3. Complementary information

Ask which uncertainty can change the decision. A measurement, a causal experiment,
a provenance check, and a value judgment solve different problems. More samples
do not settle a disagreement about authority or objectives.

A test with no standalone value can complement another test. Evaluate a small
mechanism-supported bundle when this matters; include its costs and possible side
effects. Do not enumerate arbitrary combinations. The bundle calculator's parity
model is a constructed example, not a general estimate of research value.

## 4. Higher-order effects and reflexivity

Trace first-order direct changes, second-order adaptations, third-order interacting
responses, and fourth-order changes in institutions, power, dependencies, or future
options. These are causal layers, not periods one through four.

For each material branch retain its mechanism, affected parties, evidence status,
horizon, leading indicator, disconfirming observation, and decision consequence.
Label speculative branches. Stop low-value expansion explicitly; continue beyond
four layers when a material hazard remains.

Treat a private forecast, its publication, and action based on it as distinct
interventions. Test whether apparent improvement reflects changed measurement,
shifted burdens, or altered incentives. A prevented event is not automatically a
failed original risk forecast. State limits when no credible counterfactual exists.

## 5. Operating limits and recovery

For consequential recommendations state the model's applicability: source
reliability, scenarios considered and excluded, parameter ranges, feedback delays,
verification limits, and conditions requiring reconsideration.

Fast correction can destabilize delayed feedback. A linear stability calculation
applies only to its stated recurrence, not to arbitrary social systems, switching
dynamics, or fairness. Test horizon sensitivity separately from causal-order depth.

A recovery plan needs actual authority, capacity, time, and feasible interfaces.
Resource margin arithmetic does not reserve resources or prove recoverability.
Do not create a fictitious emergency allowance. Stopping an ongoing process can
itself cause harm; do not prescribe shutdown without considering its authorized
continuation and recovery conditions.

## 6. Dissent and correction

Use role perspectives only when they contribute different falsifiers or checks.
Record shared sources and unresolved disagreement. Neither more personas nor
unanimous generated opinions constitute independent evidence.

When evidence changes, invalidate dependent conclusions and reassess. A new source
string is not verification. Retain unmeasured outcomes and attribution limits.
No performance multiplier, deployment safety claim, or expert-equivalence claim
follows from the presence of this workflow or from passing its software tests.
