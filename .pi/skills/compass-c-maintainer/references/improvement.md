# Repo skill improvement intake

## Method and ownership

This package adopts the Agent Skill Engineer method, not its entire implementation.
Reviewed source: `tryingET/procesio-cli`, commit
`ef56eeecdc21a153c91b047af0e02c0b68924a4c`, skill version 2.0.1 under
`skills/agent-skill-engineer/`. Relevant source-owned references are
`decision-framework.md`, `authoring-standard.md` and `evaluation-standard.md`.
Consult the current owner source before substantive skill optimization; this pin
records the adoption baseline, not an automatic update channel.

COMPASS-C owns this consumer skill and its regression evidence. KES/source repos
own learning; AK owns accepted task/evidence/knowledge state where landed. No
additional learning database or auto-promoter is implemented by this package.

## Evidence to intervention

1. Obtain a permitted, sanitized observation: repeated failure, explicit operator
   correction, costly risk, or accepted learning with source identity and scope.
   A session summary is a candidate signal, not an accepted learning by itself.
2. Identify the causal layer: need/ownership, domain model, discovery, instructions,
   resources, tool/runtime, observation or evaluation. Prefer a type, schema,
   validator or regression test when it can enforce the requirement.
3. Inspect recipient applicability and conflicting/superseding guidance. An
   agent-personal, society, company, project, frame, wave or task scope does not
   grant access to it. Keep private human memory and held-out evaluation material
   out of skill bodies and public fixtures.
4. Propose one source-owned intervention with baseline commit, target files,
   changed behavior, counterexample, regression protection and withdrawal rule.
   Record the work in the owning task. Do not create an independent status ledger.
5. Freeze the evaluation contract before formal optimization. Existing cases here
   are author-visible development cases, not an untouched holdout. Skill authors
   may inspect them; a future response-producing evaluation candidate must not
   receive its hidden grading criteria.
6. For an integrity defect, demonstrate failure then repair and regressions. For
   improved reasoning/selection claims, use the methodology's frozen host, A/A
   noise checks, paired baseline/candidate evidence, holdouts and fixed thresholds.
   Static checks cannot establish those outcomes.
7. Obtain recipient acceptance before adoption. Record exact adopted revision,
   applicable scope and validation in existing owner evidence. Fresh-host discovery
   and actual use are separate observations, not implied by committed files.
8. On contradiction, drift or regression, stop recommending the affected revision,
   identify impacted consumers and propose withdrawal/supersession through their
   owners. Preserve prior evidence. Do not erase history or overwrite installed
   skills as a rollback shortcut.

## Multi-order checks

- Direct: does the change repair the actual decision or executable failure?
- Adaptation: does it steal another skill's triggers or inflate every session?
- Propagation: could a local preference become incorrectly universal via templates?
- Feedback: could contaminated cases, self-grading or shared-source juries falsely
  reward the change? Rejected edits and failed outcomes remain evidence.

Fleet/template adoption is a later consumer-owned operation. A successful first
consumer is a basis for an adoption proposal, not authority to modify other repos.
