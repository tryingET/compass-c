# Purpose

COMPASS-C owns conditional decision comparison and revisable evidence records.

In scope:

- a portable `compass` Agent Skill;
- bounded deterministic decision calculations;
- local task-owned SQLite records with dependency invalidation;
- CLI, packaging, installation, and optional adapter surfaces for those capabilities;
- evidence contracts for evaluating skill routing and behavior.

Out of scope:

- autonomous action authorization or unrestricted execution;
- replacing domain specialists, research tools, or governance owners;
- presenting generated personas as real expert validation;
- hidden background learning or automatic world monitoring;
- treating structural completeness as truth, safety, or approval.
